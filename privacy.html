Privacy Policy

For Thirty · Effective November 25, 2026

Thirty keeps your journal on your device. We do not operate servers to collect your app data, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Sign in with Apple

When you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. Thirty stores this sign-in record locally on your device. Your name may prefill your editable Profile name. Thirty does not receive your Apple password.

Apple processes the sign-in request under Apple’s privacy policy. Thirty does not send your journal to Apple as part of signing in.

What stays on your device

Your rituals, daily checks, highlights, profile, and app preferences are stored locally using AsyncStorage. Thirty does not upload this information to our servers or provide cloud sync. Signing in does not back up your journal.

Local storage is not a cloud backup or an encrypted vault. Your device’s operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings; those backups are managed by your device or backup provider, not by Thirty.

No ads, analytics, or data sales

Thirty has no analytics, ads, or tracking. We do not sell or share your app data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. We do not receive your locally stored rituals, checks, highlights, or profile.

Deleting your information

  • Sign out: the Sign out link in Profile removes only the local sign-in record. It keeps your rituals, checks, highlights, and profile so they are still there when you return.
  • Delete local app data: delete the app from your device, including its app data. On iPhone, use Delete App, not Offload App, which keeps data. Device backups may retain copies; manage or delete those separately through your backup settings.

Because we do not hold your journal on a server, we cannot recover it or remotely delete it for you.

Children’s privacy

Thirty is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you have a concern about a child’s use of Thirty, please contact us.

If you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or sensitive journal entries.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. If we introduce cloud backup or sync in the future, we will update this policy to explain what is stored, where it goes, and your choices before introducing that functionality.
