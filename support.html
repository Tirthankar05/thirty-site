A few common questions

Where is my data stored?

Your rituals, checks, highlights, profile, and sign-in record are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. Thirty has no journal servers, analytics, or tracking. Read our Privacy Policy for more.

Will my journal move to a new phone?

Thirty does not currently offer cloud backup or sync. Sign in with Apple does not restore or sync your journal. Your operating system’s device backup or transfer may preserve app data, but Thirty cannot guarantee or recover it. Keep a separate copy of anything important before changing phones or deleting the app.

How do I delete my data?

Signing out in Profile removes only your sign-in record; your journal and profile stay on the device. To remove all local Thirty data, delete the app and its data. On iPhone, choose Delete App rather than Offload App. Any device backups must be managed separately in your backup settings.

Does Thirty send reminders?

Not yet. The daily reminder setting currently saves your preference only; it does not schedule phone notifications. There is no need to change notification permissions to use the journal.
